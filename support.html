Account and work support

Contact your contractor administrator for account approval, delivery-centre assignments, inactive access, compliance documents, work records, invoice decisions, fines or complaint workflows.

Technical and privacy support

For a reproducible app problem or a privacy request, email seraphcodetech@gmail.com. Include your account email, device type, app version and a short description. Do not email passwords, bank account numbers, identity documents or other sensitive evidence.

Urgent safety matters

Do not rely on the app for emergency assistance. Call Australian emergency services on 000 when immediate police, fire or ambulance assistance is required, then follow your contractor’s incident process when safe.
