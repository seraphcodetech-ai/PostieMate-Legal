Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

Who this policy covers

Seraph Code PTY LTD provides PostieMate Driver to authorised delivery contractors and their drivers. Your contractor administrator controls your work account and may also be responsible for handling your personal information under its own privacy obligations.

Information we collect

We process account and contact details; driver profile, licence, work-rights and Fit2Work information; contractor, ABN, GST, bank and invoice details; delivery runs and parcel counts; vehicle inspections; complaints, fines and incident records; signatures; photos and uploaded documents; device and notification identifiers; app version and security settings; and crash and diagnostic information needed to maintain reliability.

Location information

When you start an active delivery run, PostieMate collects precise location data, including while the app is in the background or closed. Location points may be held securely on the device while offline and uploaded when a connection returns. Tracking stops when the run is completed. Location is used for delivery operations, safety, route records and complaint resolution. It is not used for advertising.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate drivers, confirm work eligibility, operate delivery and safety workflows, create and approve invoices, provide notifications and support, prevent misuse, meet legal and contractual duties, and improve reliability and security.

Who can receive information

Information is available only to authorised users such as your contractor administrator, assigned delivery-centre personnel and authorised PostieMate administrators. Service providers supporting hosting, file storage, authentication, diagnostics and notifications process data under controlled access. PostieMate does not sell personal information.

Storage, security and retention

Production application data and files are protected using access controls, encryption and audit logging. Data is retained for operational, contractual and legal record-keeping periods, then deleted or de-identified when it is no longer required. Notification and diagnostic providers may process limited device information outside Australia.

Your choices and requests

You can control camera, notification, biometric and location permissions in device settings. Some work functions cannot operate without required permissions. Contact your contractor administrator or PostieMate support to request access, correction or deletion. Some records may need to be kept where the law or a contract requires it.

Contact and changes

Questions, complaints and privacy requests can be sent to seraphcodetech@gmail.com. We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
